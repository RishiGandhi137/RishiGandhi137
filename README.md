## Research

**Aharonov-Bohm transport in toroidal quantum dots with Gabriel's horn heterostructures**

I am a research intern at WPI's Center for Computational NanoScience, working under Professor L. Ramdas Ram-Mohan. The project uses finite element methods to study how electrons move through quantum dots shaped as a torus and how magnetic flux affects that transport.
